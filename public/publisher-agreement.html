Key Terms — At a Glance

  • You are an independent contractor (not an employee)

  • Credits are promotional credit redeemable only for advertising services on the Boost Boss network — no cash value, non-transferable, never redeemable for money
  • Boost Boss does not make cash payments to publishers
  • You are responsible for your own taxes in your jurisdiction

1. Parties and scope

This Publisher Agreement ("Agreement") is between Hauler Technologies Co., Ltd., operating as Boost Boss ("Boost Boss"), and you, the publisher ("Publisher," "you"). It supplements the Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, and Privacy Policy, all of which apply.

2. Independent contractor

You are an independent contractor. This Agreement does not create an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship. You have no authority to bind Boost Boss. You are solely responsible for your own taxes, benefits, insurance, and legal compliance in your jurisdiction of residence and operation.

3. Inventory representation

You represent that:

  • You own or have the right to monetize the application(s) in which you integrate Boost Boss ad units
  • Your inventory category, audience description, and traffic figures provided during onboarding are accurate
  • Your application(s) comply with applicable laws in all jurisdictions where they are accessed
  • You will not misrepresent inventory through cloaking, bot traffic, or any other manipulation

4. Ad delivery and integration

You will integrate Boost Boss ads using one of the four official publisher SDKs — Lumi for Browser App, Browser Extension App, Computer App, or Mobile App — or another integration path expressly approved by Boost Boss. You will:

  • Render ad markup faithfully, without modification
  • Not block, hide, position over, or obscure rendered ads
  • Comply with our brand‑safety, frequency capping, and viewability rules
  • Not place ads on content that violates our Acceptable Use Policy
  • Maintain accurate ads.txt or app‑ads.txt records identifying Boost Boss as a permitted seller

4.1 Automatic ad placement

By installing the Lumi SDK on any of the four integration surfaces (Computer App, Browser App, Browser Extension App, Mobile App), you authorize Boost Boss to automatically render its full set of placements ("auto‑mount") into reasonable locations within your application's user interface. The Lumi runtime uses heuristic detection of common UI patterns (chat containers, suggestion bars, feed lists, empty states, etc.) and falls back to safe default locations (e.g., a corner of the viewport) when an ideal placement cannot be confidently detected.

You acknowledge and agree that:

  • Placement decisions are made automatically by the Lumi runtime; specific placement locations may vary across page loads, sessions, and SDK versions
  • You can suppress any individual placement by adding a data-lumi-disable="<placement>" attribute or, on supported surfaces, declaring an explicit slot location via data-lumi-slot. Disabling placements reduces your impression volume and the Credits you earn.
  • Boost Boss will not knowingly place ads inside structural risky containers (code blocks, password fields, system messages, navigation chrome, or other elements likely to break user experience)
  • If a placement causes a genuine user‑experience or compliance issue in your application, you will notify support@boostboss.ai and we will collaborate to suppress or relocate the placement
  • You remain solely responsible for ensuring that automatically‑rendered Boost Boss ads, as a category, are consistent with your own user‑facing terms of service and privacy policy

5. Credits

5.1 Accrual

For each valid ad impression delivered through your application(s), your account accrues one (1) Credit. Credit accrual is Boost Boss's sole form of publisher compensation under this Agreement.

5.2 Nature of Credits

Credits are promotional credit redeemable only for advertising services on the Boost Boss network. Credits have no cash value, are non-transferable, and are not redeemable for money or any other monetary payment. Boost Boss does not make cash payments to publishers under any circumstances, including on termination of this Agreement. Credits are not property, currency, a deposit, a security, or a stored-value instrument, and confer no right other than the right to redeem them for advertising services on the network subject to this Agreement.

5.3 Adjustments and reversals

Credits attributable to invalid traffic, fraud, or violations of this Agreement or the Acceptable Use Policy may be withheld, reversed, or forfeited. Boost Boss may adjust the accrual rate or redemption terms with at least 30 days' notice for prospective impressions.

6. Reporting and disputes

Impression, click, and Credit figures shown in the Boost Boss dashboard are the official record for Credit accrual. You must raise any Credit dispute within 30 days of the relevant period; later disputes are waived.

7. Taxes

Credits are promotional credit with no cash value and are not monetary compensation. You are nonetheless solely responsible for determining and complying with any tax consequences that may apply to your participation in the Services in your jurisdiction.

8. Sanctions and prohibited jurisdictions

You may not access the Services or accrue or redeem Credits if you reside in, are located in, or are a national of a country subject to U.S. or international sanctions (including Iran, North Korea, Cuba, Crimea, Syria, and other sanctioned regions). You represent that you are not on any sanctions list.

9. Brand safety and removal

We may remove ads from, suspend ad delivery to, or terminate inventory of any publisher application that:

  • Violates our Acceptable Use Policy
  • Generates a high volume of invalid traffic or fraud
  • Damages advertiser brand safety
  • Generates a disproportionate volume of advertiser complaints
  • Operates in a manner inconsistent with industry standards (IAB, MRC)

10. Use of marks

You may use the name "Boost Boss" and our logo solely to identify your partnership with us, subject to our brand guidelines (available on request). You may not register or use any confusingly similar name, domain, or social handle.

11. Confidentiality

Non‑public information about the Services, including pricing structures, traffic statistics not pertaining to your own inventory, technical interfaces, and roadmap, is confidential. You may not disclose it without our written consent.

12. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time. On termination, accrued but unredeemed Credits are forfeited. Credits have no cash value, and no payment or other compensation is due to you on termination.

We may suspend you immediately for breach, fraud, or to comply with law. Sections that by their nature survive termination (taxes, IP, confidentiality, indemnification, governing law) shall continue in effect.

13. Liability

The Services are provided "as is." Our total liability for any claim under this Agreement shall not exceed US $100. We are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

14. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Taiwan (R.O.C.). Disputes shall be addressed first by good‑faith negotiation; if unresolved within 60 days, by the Taipei District Court (台北地方法院). Class actions are not permitted.

15. Changes

We may update this Agreement with at least 14 days' notice. Continued use of the Services after the effective date constitutes acceptance. Pricing changes apply only to prospective transactions.
