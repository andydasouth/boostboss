# Boost Boss — The User-Acquisition Network for AI Products

**Status:** Strategy of record (2026-07-04). Extends [strategy-free-launch-network.md](./strategy-free-launch-network.md) with competitive validation, the moat mechanic, and the onboarding funnel.

---

## 1. The position, in one line

**The user-acquisition network for AI products — list your product, get in front of people already using AI tools.**

Not *"monetize your AI app"* (everyone does that). *"Get users for your AI product"* (no one does).

We are AppLovin for AI, not AdSense for AI. The earning side (Boosts) is the *fuel*; the *product* is user acquisition.

---

## 2. Why this wins — competitive verdict

Deep competitive teardown (Jul 2026) confirmed the hypothesis: **every funded player is a supply-side monetization pipe.** They all pitch *"earn money from your AI app,"* and fill demand with conventional brands (Nike, Walmart, Upwork) — not with other AI apps seeking users.

| Player | Pitch | Center of gravity | Loop or pipe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Koah** (~$26M, Theory Ventures) | "AdSense for GenAI" | Supply-side monetization | Open pipe — **the real threat** |
| **Imprezia** (YC S25, ~$500K) | "Monetization Infrastructure for AI" | Supply-side | Open exchange, invite-only |
| **Elo** (Pear VC) | "Adserver for AI chat… get paid" | Supply-side | Invite-only pipe |
| **AgentVine** | "Ad network for AI agents" | Demand-*framed*, dev-monetization | Pre-traction pipe |
| **Nexad** ($6M, a16z) | "GTM for the AI era" | Demand-first — but supply = big assistants, buyers = normal brands | Wrong loop |
| ChatAds / ZeroClick / ProRata / Dappier | monetize AI traffic | Supply-side | Cash CPM / rev-share |

**The empty quadrant:** demand-first + no-gatekeeping supply + intent-quality matching + a **closed credit loop where every dev is both promoter and inventory.** Nobody occupies it.

**The clock:** Koah is two-sided, funded, and owns inventory — one positioning reframe away from being us with more money. We move first, and we go where they won't (see §7).

---

## 3. The moat — the *enforced* loop (AppLovin's real secret)

AppLovin's moat was never the tech. It's that **you can't buy installs without also selling inventory** (per Eric Seufert). That binding forces every participant onto both sides, so the data and the value never leak out of the loop. AXON and MAX are beneficiaries of the loop, not the source of the advantage.

**Boost Boss rule:** to promote *for free*, you must *host*. Cash is the only way to promote without hosting.

- Host ads (install SDK) → earn Boosts → spend Boosts to acquire users. Every dev is both sides.
- Free distribution is *earned* by contributing inventory; cash *amplifies*.
- This keeps the audience pure (AI-native users only) and the value recirculating inside the network.

This single rule converts our "nice flywheel" into AppLovin's actual moat. **Make it structural, not optional.**

## 4. The onboarding funnel — Product-Hunt-simple, demand-first

The current `boostboss.ai` "See your ad on all 37 placements — instantly" form is the seed of the real signup. Turn it into the entry.

**Step 1 — List your product (least friction).**
Like Product Hunt: Logo, Company name, one-line description, URL. That's it. No SDK, no creative upload, no credit card to start. Minimal creative on purpose.

**Step 2 — Free taste.**
Your product immediately runs on **3–5 placements** — real AI users see it. This is the hook: proof it works before any commitment.

**Step 3 — Unlock all 37 placements (millions of surfaces).**
To go from 3–5 → the full 37, the dev must:
1. **Upload more creative** in the console, **and**
2. **Install the SDK** → host ads → earn Boosts → *now you also make money.*

Step 3 **is the enforced loop as a growth funnel.** The unlock (full distribution) is gated on becoming supply. Cash is the alternate amplifier for anyone who won't host.

> Design mandate: keep every step as simple as humanly possible. The signup friction is the enemy. Creative minimal, copy minimal, one clear action per step.

---

## 5. Anti-Product-Hunt design principles

Product Hunt is dying from vanity mechanics (launch-day upvotes), an opaque ~10%-featured gate, and insider "hunter" capture — it became a badge, not a growth channel. TAAFT is beating it by selling *evergreen intent traffic* instead of a one-day spike. Lessons, baked in:

- **Rank on users actually delivered, never on votes.** No upvote game to capture.
- **Continuous distribution, not a launch-day spike.** Evergreen placement, always on.
- **Keep the newcomer path open.** No insider capture; a brand-new dev can get real reach day one.
- **Ranking is intent + performance, transparent.** Optimize delivered value, not a gameable metric.

---

## 6. Guardrails — where we say no

- **No gatekeeping on quality or DAU.** Embrace the long tail — that's the supply engine.
- **Gatekeep hard on vertical fit:** AI products acquiring AI-native users. No off-vertical spam (crypto casinos, adult, iGaming). That "no" *is* the brand and the defense against becoming PropellerAds.
- **Auditability from day one.** AppLovin now faces an SEC probe and self-dealing accusations precisely because outsiders can't verify lift inside its closed loop. We're a closed loop too — so honest causation, real incrementality, and transparent per-ad analytics are load-bearing, not bolted on. Don't become the bear case.

---

## 7. The long-tail wedge

Koah's publishers are real apps with millions of MAU. They will never court a 50-user Lovable app or an indie MCP tool. **That's our ocean:** the thousands of tiny AI apps spun up weekly beneath every funded network's floor. Zero-gatekeeping + the starter Boost grant is the acquisition engine for exactly that tail. We don't fight Koah for the whales; we aggregate the 10,000 minnows.

---

## 8. The honest risk — funny money

Every network is supply-heavy because *who actually pays* is the unsolved problem. Our credit loop is the **only** model that manufactures demand without needing cash advertisers to start — that's the structural edge. But credits are funny money until real cash enters.

- **Milestone that proves the thesis:** first real cash advertiser / first cash→Boost conversion at scale.
- Cash outranks credit in the auction (already built) so real demand always wins priority.
- Accept the funny-money phase as intentional bootstrap, not a flaw — it's how we skip the cold-start every two-sided network dies on.

---

## 9. Build implications (near-term)

1. **Reframe the front door.** `boostboss.ai` still runs the old two-sided nav (Publishers / SuperBoost Ads / BBX). Collapse to the single demand-first story: *"List your AI product. Get users. Free."*
2. **Turn the landing form into the signup.** Logo + name + one-line + URL → account + 3–5 live placements. Least friction.
3. **Console = the unlock.** Creative upload + SDK install lifts 3–5 → 37. Surface the Boost balance as the currency and the "install SDK to earn + unlock" prompt as the primary upsell.
4. **Enforce the loop.** Full free distribution requires hosting (or cash). Wire the gate.
